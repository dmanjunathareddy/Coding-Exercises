Given a large dataset, how would you design an efficient query to aggregate data?
SELECT 
    product_id, 
    region, 
    SUM(amount) AS total_sales, 
    AVG(amount) AS average_sales, 
    COUNT(*) AS number_of_sales
FROM 
    sales
WHERE 
    date BETWEEN '2023-01-01' AND '2023-12-31'
GROUP BY 
    product_id, region
ORDER BY 
    total_sales DESC;
